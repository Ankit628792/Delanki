# Love Alarm 2.0 — Expressing Affection in the Real World with Dignity & Consent

> *"Telling someone you like them is one of the most natural human feelings, yet one of the hardest to say out loud. Love Alarm turns private hesitation into a gentle, reversible signal."*

---

## 1. What is Love Alarm 2.0?

**Love Alarm 2.0** is a proximity-based social application inspired by the popular webtoon concept. It gives people a gentle way to express romantic affection without the fear or awkwardness of an uninvited verbal confession.

When two people pass within approximately 10 meters of each other in the real world (such as on a campus, in a favorite café, or along a shared commute), and one has chosen to ring the other's alarm, a quiet notification alerts the recipient that someone nearby has feelings for them. 

---

## 2. The Problem It Solves

### The Heavy Burden of First Words
* **The Fear of Rejection:** Millions of people share classrooms, offices, or neighborhood spots with someone they admire, but freeze up when trying to say hello.
* **Awkward Misunderstandings:** Cold confessions can feel overwhelming or risk ruining an existing friendship.
* **Endless Swiping vs. Real Life:** Most modern dating apps focus on endless photos of strangers across a whole city. Love Alarm 2.0 focuses on the people who already share your real physical environment.

### The Breakthrough: Choice, Proximity, and Consent
Earlier ideas relied on fictional wearables or automated mind-reading. The real human solution is much simpler:
> **You don't need a gadget to measure heartbeats. You just need to honor a conscious human choice.**

With Love Alarm 2.0:
1. **You choose who to ring.** No algorithm guesses for you.
2. **Proximity matters.** The signal only activates when you are actually in the same small radius (10 meters)—the exact moment when a smile or greeting could happen.
3. **Consent is always in control.** Anyone can pause their alarm, remove a ring, or block unwanted rings with a single tap.

---

## 3. How It Works (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Tap to Ring (A Quiet Intention)
When you notice someone you like on campus or in your community, you tap the heart to ring their alarm. You don't have to walk over or deliver a speech. It is a quiet intention for the future.

### Step 2: Going About Your Day
Life goes on—classes, studying, coffee breaks. Nothing happens until your paths physically cross in the real world.

### Step 3: The 10-Meter Proximity Alert
When you and the person come within 10 meters of each other, their alarm chimes softly. Their screen shows that someone nearby likes them. The moment is gentle: no spotlights, no public embarrassment, just a spark of awareness.

### Step 4: Mutual Rings Unlock Conversation
If both people have chosen to ring each other's alarms, a **Mutual Match** occurs! At this point, the app unlocks direct messaging, allowing a comfortable conversation to begin with mutual confidence.

### Step 5: Full Control Anytime
Feelings change, and comfort comes first. You can cancel a ring anytime from your history. If you don't want someone ringing you, you can dismiss them or block them permanently. You can also pause your alarm whenever you need personal quiet.
