# Respira — Reclaiming Calm and Focus Through Conscious Breathing

> *"We take over 20,000 breaths a day, yet almost never with intention. In a world of notifications and deadlines, the simple act of breathing is our quickest path back to calm."*

---

## 1. What is Respira?

**Respira** is a calm, distraction-free breathing assistant and respiratory wellness companion. It helps you relieve stress, reset your focus during a hectic workday, drift off to sleep naturally, and improve your lung endurance.

Unlike noisy meditation apps filled with long audio lectures, complex subscription popups, or social leaderboards, Respira focuses purely on how your breath interacts with your body. In just four minutes, its gentle visual guide, soft acoustic chimes, and calming vibrations help your body shift out of "fight or flight" mode and back into balance.

---

## 2. The Problem It Solves

### The Modern Trap of "Screen Apnea"
Knowledge workers, healthcare professionals, students, and busy parents face a subtle, overlooked issue every day:
* **Holding Your Breath at Screens:** When reading an urgent email, tackling code, or multitasking, people unconsciously hold their breath or breathe very shallowly from the upper chest.
* **Brain Fog & Tension:** By 3 PM, this lack of steady oxygen leaves you feeling mentally drained, irritable, and physically tight across the neck and shoulders.
* **Meditation Can Feel Intimidating:** Sitting still in silence to "clear your mind" can feel impossible when anxiety is running high. People need a concrete, physical tool they can feel immediately.

### The Breakthrough Moment
You cannot consciously tell your liver to work faster or command your heart rate to drop by sheer willpower. But **breathing is the one bodily rhythm you can control with your mind.**

When you slow down your exhale and rhythmically expand your lungs, you directly stimulate the vagus nerve. This instantly tells your heart and brain: *"You are safe. You can relax."*

## 3. How It Works (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Arrive at a Quiet Sanctuary
Respira opens to an understated, dark obsidian screen that is easy on tired eyes. There are no sudden splash screens or ads. You can start breathing within one second.

### Step 2: Choose Your Breathing Goal
Exercises are organized by real human needs:
* **Box Breathing (4-4-4-4):** Equal inhale, hold, exhale, and rest. Used by first responders and athletes to regain composure before high-stakes tasks.
* **4-7-8 Sleep Flow:** A long, gentle exhale that triggers natural drowsiness before bedtime.
* **Coherent Resonance (5.5-5.5):** Smooth, flowing breath that balances cardiovascular rhythm and reduces stress.
* **Lung Capacity Self-Test:** A gentle way to measure how long you can comfortably hold your breath, helping track respiratory improvement over time.

### Step 3: Eyes-Closed Practice
You don't even have to look at the screen! Gentle harmonic bells and soft vibration pulses signal when to inhale, hold, and exhale, allowing you to close your eyes and rest.

### Step 4: Track Meaningful Progress
Check your daily mindful minutes and vital hold capacity without stressful streak guilt.
