# AirBeam-Share — Sending Files Without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Cloud Servers

> *"What happens when you need to send a private file, but you cannot afford to trust the network or reveal your location?"*

---

## 1. What is AirBeam-Share?

**AirBeam-Share** is an off-grid, optical file transfer application that moves sensitive documents, photos, and notes between two nearby devices using **nothing more than pulses of visible light**.

One screen flashes a dynamic, high-density stream of optical QR patterns, and the camera lens on the second device scans it. In seconds, the file is assembled and saved directly to the device—with **zero Wi-Fi, zero Bluetooth, and zero internet connection**.

---

## 2. The Problem It Solves

### The Invisible Risks of Wireless Sharing
Today, sending even a tiny document across a room usually requires radio waves:
* **Discoverable Radio Signals:** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth broadcast signals that can be detected, logged, or intercepted by nearby network scanners.
* **Unreliable in Remote Areas:** Basements, underground tunnels, flights, boats at sea, and disaster zones often have zero signal and no routers.
* **Cloud Dependence:** Many wireless sharing tools secretly send files through a remote cloud server before delivering them to someone sitting right across the table.
* **Complex Pairing Frustrations:** "Why isn't my phone seeing your device?" and "Are we on the same Wi-Fi?" are questions we've all asked.

### The Breakthrough Moment
Human eyes don't need a Wi-Fi password or a network handshake to read a book across a table. 

If human beings can share ideas simply by looking at ink on paper, why can't our phones exchange data the same way? By turning digital files into high-speed optical patterns on a screen, devices can communicate through pure light.

---

## 3. How It Works (Step by Step)

AirBeam-Share is completely air-gapped and requires no accounts, phone numbers, or passwords:

### Step 1: Select Your File (The Sender)
Pick a document, photo, or note. You can adjust the light pulse speed to match room brightness, or add an optional verbal password.

### Step 2: Cast the Light Beam
With a tap, your phone screen becomes a visual beacon, flashing high-contrast, animated optical blocks that represent the file.

### Step 3: Catch the Light (The Receiver)
The recipient opens AirBeam-Share and points their camera lens at the sender's screen. No Bluetooth discovery, no pairing pins. A live puzzle grid on the receiver's screen fills up in real time as each optical block is captured.

### Step 4: Instant Confirmation & Local Save
A friendly haptic vibration confirms the transfer is 100% complete and verified. The file is saved directly into the phone's private storage. It never touches a cloud server.

## 4. Key Highlights

* **100% Air-Gapped & Zero Radio Waves:** Works flawlessly in Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned off.
* **Visible Transparency:** You can literally watch the data move. If you cover the screen or point the camera away, the transfer stops immediately.
* **Zero Intermediaries:** No cloud accounts, no phone numbers, no tracking cookies, and no network logs.
* **Local Room Database:** Encrypted local history stored safely on your device.
